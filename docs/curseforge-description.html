Cassette

Control your music from inside WoW: Spotify, or YouTube Music and Apple Music in beta. A compact player with the song that's playing, its cover, a live progress bar and the controls, right in your UI.

  • Stays in sync: song, artist and cover change the moment your music does.
  • Play, pause, skip, shuffle and volume with a click.
  • Spotify, YouTube Music or Apple Music: YouTube Music works in your browser, its installed app or a desktop app; Apple Music in its app or iTunes. YouTube Music and Apple Music are in beta, so they may not work fully yet.
  • Your Spotify: playlists, liked songs, and search for songs, albums and artists, in a scrolling browser. The song that's playing is highlighted.
  • Quieter during dialogue: with Spoken, your music turns down while NPCs talk, and back up when they finish.
  • Highly customisable: five layouts, three themes (Classic, Modern, EllesmereUI), any accent colour (including your class colour), plus adjustable size and background opacity.

Planned features

  • Playlists and search for YouTube Music and Apple Music: for now, these work with Spotify only.

You also need the Cassette app (Windows)

WoW addons can't talk to your music app on their own, so Cassette comes with a small app that runs in your system tray. Download it from GitHub: github.com/ghilliepng/Cassette/releases/latest It tells you in game when there's a new version, and updates itself with one click.

When you run the installer, Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC". That's because Cassette is free and isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. All of Cassette's code is public on GitHub, and each release lists a checksum so you can check your download is the real one.

Playlists, liked songs and search are Spotify's, and need Spotify Premium. The player works with any account. With YouTube Music in a browser, the volume is the whole browser's, and shuffle stays in YouTube Music.

Right-click the player, or type /cassette options, for settings. Not affiliated with Spotify, Google, Apple or Blizzard.
