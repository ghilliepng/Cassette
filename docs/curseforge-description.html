Cassette

Control Spotify from inside WoW. A compact player with the song that's playing, its cover, a live progress bar and the controls, right in your UI.

  • Always up to date: song, artist and cover change the moment Spotify does.
  • Play, pause, skip, shuffle and volume with a click.
  • Your music: playlists, liked songs, and search for songs, albums and artists, in a scrolling browser. The song that's playing is highlighted.
  • Quieter during dialogue: with Spoken, your music turns down while NPCs talk, and back up when they finish.
  • Highly customisable: five layouts, three themes (Classic, Modern, EllesmereUI), any accent colour (including your class colour), plus adjustable size and background opacity.

You also need the Cassette app (Windows)

WoW addons can't talk to Spotify on their own, so Cassette comes with a small app that runs in your system tray. Download it from GitHub: github.com/ghilliepng/Cassette/releases/latest It tells you in game when there's a new version, and updates itself with one click.

When you run the installer, Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC". That's because Cassette is free and isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway. All of Cassette's code is public on GitHub, and each release lists a checksum so you can check your download is the real one.

Playlists, liked songs and search need Spotify Premium; play, pause, skip and volume work with any account.

Right-click the player, or type /cassette options, for settings. Not affiliated with Spotify or Blizzard.
